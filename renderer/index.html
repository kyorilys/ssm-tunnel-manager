Settings

  

    

      
Open at login
When your Mac starts, SSM Tunnels opens in the menu bar (no window), connects the tunnels marked Auto, and keeps them up: a dropped session retries after 2s, 4s, 8s, 16s and 32s, and right away after wake from sleep.

      
      
Takes effect in the packaged app. When started with npm start, macOS can’t register it as a login item.

      

    

    

      
Archived tunnels stop retrying and leave the list and menu bar. They stay in storage; restore any time from Archived.

      
      

      
      
  • The clock starts at a tunnel’s first failure and resets once it stays connected for 10 seconds. After 5 failed retries a tunnel stops retrying until you press Retry.
  • Days when every tunnel fails don’t count. That points to an expired AWS login or no network, not a bad tunnel.
  • Archived tunnels are deleted permanently after the chosen number of days unless you restore them.

    

    

      
Disconnect tunnels when quitting
On: quitting stops the tunnels SSM Tunnels started. Off: they keep running and appear with the Terminal badge next time. Sessions started in Terminal are never touched on quit.
