Open at login
When your Mac starts, SSM Tunnels opens in the menu bar (no window), connects the tunnels marked Auto, and keeps them up: a dropped session retries after 2s, 4s, 8s… up to every 60s, and right away after wake from sleep.

      
      

    

    

      
Archived tunnels stop retrying and leave the list and menu bar. They stay in storage; restore any time from Archived.

      
      
  • The clock starts at a tunnel’s first failure and resets once it stays connected for 10 seconds.
  • Days when every tunnel fails don’t count. That points to an expired AWS login or no network, not a bad tunnel.
