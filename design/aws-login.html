Settings

  

    
Open at login
When your Mac starts, SSM Tunnels opens in the menu bar (no window), connects the tunnels marked Auto, and keeps them up.

    
Archived tunnels stop retrying and leave the list and menu bar. Days when every tunnel fails, or tunnels wait for AWS sign-in, don’t count.

      

    
Disconnect tunnels when quitting
On: quitting stops the tunnels SSM Tunnels started. Sessions started in Terminal are never touched on quit.
